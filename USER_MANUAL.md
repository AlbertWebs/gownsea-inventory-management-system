# GOWNSEA User Manual

This guide explains how to use GOWNSEA Operations without needing technical knowledge. It covers the screens available to staff and managers. Your account may show fewer options than this guide because access depends on your role and assigned branches.

## Getting started

### Sign in

1. Open the GOWNSEA sign-in page and enter the email address and password provided for your staff account.
2. Select **Sign in**.
3. If asked, enter the current code from your authenticator app.
4. If you cannot remember your password, choose **Forgot password**, enter your account email, and follow the link sent to you.

**Note:** Password reset and staff setup links arrive by email and expire. Ask an administrator to check your account if the email does not arrive or the link has expired.

### Choose a branch

1. In a form, open the **Location** or **Branch** selector.
2. Choose where the stock, customer, payment, or event belongs.
3. If the branch you need is missing, ask a manager to review your access. Staff can only see information for branches assigned to their account.

### Stay connected

1. Check the top bar before submitting an important change. **Offline** means the app cannot reach the server.
2. Wait until the **Offline** notice disappears before saving stock, hire, return, or payment changes.
3. After submitting, check that the page confirms the change was saved.

## Dashboard and navigation

1. Read the dashboard cards for stock availability, current hires, returns due today, overdue returns, and recent payments.
2. Select a summary card or shortcut to open the related work area.
3. Change the location filter if you want to review another branch you can access. The figures follow your branch access and selected location.
4. Use the left sidebar to open **Inventory**, **Hiring & Returns**, **Customers**, **CRM**, **Orders**, **Locations & Transfers**, **Payments**, **Finance**, **Reports**, **Graduation Events**, or **User Manual**. Select an expandable item to show its subpages. Managers may also see **Institutions**, **Roles & Permissions**, and **Settings**.
5. On a narrow screen, use the menu button in the top bar to open or close the sidebar.
6. To search, enter at least two characters in the box at the top and choose a result. You can search gown barcodes, customers, orders, or hires. Results only show records you can access.
7. Select the bell to open the hiring and returns queue. Open the account menu for security settings or sign out.

### Words you will see

- **Serialized gown:** one physical gown with its own barcode.
- **Bulk stock:** several identical items tracked as a quantity rather than by separate barcodes.
- **Available:** ready to issue or transfer from the selected branch.
- **Awaiting inspection:** physically returned but not yet checked and released for use.
- **Pending:** submitted and waiting for a review or other step.
- **Branch/location:** the warehouse or shop responsible for the item or record.

## Customers and customer leads

### Customer directory

1. Open **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. Search by a customer name, phone number, email, or institution.
3. Select a customer to review their contact and hire history, or select **New customer** to add a profile.
4. Enter the customer’s name, contact details, institution if known, and the branch responsible for the record.
5. Save the profile. Kenyan mobile numbers should be entered in a form the screen accepts; the app normalizes local numbers.

6. Open the saved customer profile to review their hire activity.
7. If the text-message consent control is available, turn it on only after the customer agrees to receive messages. Turning it off stops future messages.

### CRM leads

Use **CRM** to follow a potential sale or hire before it becomes a customer transaction.

1. Select **Add a Lead** to open the lead form.
2. Enter the contact name and any available phone or email. If this person is already in the customer directory, choose them in **Existing customer** to link the records.
3. Choose an institution, branch, lead source, stage, estimated value, and any next action or follow-up date.
4. Save the lead. It appears in the pipeline board.
5. Move a card between stages by dragging it, or use the stage control on the card. The totals update to match the pipeline.
6. Open a card to record a conversation, schedule or complete a follow-up, update details, or convert a successful lead.

7. Open the lead and select **Conversations** to record a call, email, meeting, or note.
8. If the lead will not proceed, move it to **Lost** and provide a reason if requested.
9. When the lead is ready to become a customer, select **Convert to customer**. Check whether an existing customer should be linked first.

### Quotations

Open **Quotations** from the CRM page to prepare an offer for a lead.

1. Start a quotation for an open lead and add the products or services, quantities, prices, and any notes or expiry date.
2. Save the draft and review the line items and total.
3. If you change the price or offer after approval, send the revised version for approval again.
4. An authorized manager reviews the quotation. When approved, download its PDF or send it to the customer where the available contact details allow.
5. Record the customer's acceptance or decline. Accepted quotations can be converted using the fulfillment option that matches the work: custom order, bulk hire, serialized hire, or bulk sale.

**Tip:** Confirm stock type and branch before converting an accepted quotation. Serialized hire conversion requires the exact quoted gown count to be scanned. Bulk sales are recorded as paid sales; use the payment details shown by the screen.

## Quotations and custom orders

### Custom orders

Open **Orders → Orders** to see made-to-order work. Select **Create custom order** to start a new order.

1. Select the customer and responsible branch.
2. Enter a clear order description, garment details, quantities, measurements, agreed total, required deposit, due date, and delivery method.
3. Review the summary and save the order.
4. Open the order to update its progress, add production tasks, schedule fittings, record direct costs, or add delivery and courier updates.
5. When changing the order status or production details, enter the reason requested by the form. The order history shows what changed.
6. Record customer receipts in **Payments → Receipts**. The required deposit field describes what is due; it does not record that money was received.

7. Add production tasks such as cutting, sewing, fitting, alteration, quality checks, or packaging.
8. Assign a person and due date where the screen allows.
9. Record fitting attendance and notes.
10. After dispatch, update the delivery status and tracking details.

**Warning:** An order with an unrefunded deposit may not be cancellable. Process the refund through Payments first. Do not enter measurements as code or symbols; use the measurement fields and guidance shown on the order form.

## Inventory and stock

### Serialized gown inventory

1. Open **Inventory → Inventory**.
2. Search by name, SKU, barcode, condition, location, or availability.
3. Select an asset code to see its product, condition, current custody, and movement history.
4. Use the **Actions** column to choose an action available for your role and that item.

To register stock:

1. Select **Register stock**.
2. Enter the product name, SKU, category, tracking choice, size or other requested details, and purchase information if requested.
3. Choose the branch receiving the stock.
4. For serialized gowns, scan or enter each unique asset code. Check the number of codes against the physical count.
5. Save the product and confirm that the listed quantity and codes match the items received.

1. Select the assets that need labels.
2. Choose **Print labels**.
3. Print one test sheet and check its scale and label stock before printing a larger batch.

**Warning:** A barcode identifies a physical gown. Check it carefully before issuing, returning, transferring, or writing off an item. Do not reuse a code for a different gown.

### Bulk stock

Choose **Inventory → Bulk Stock** for products tracked by quantity rather than individual barcodes.

1. Select a product and the branch receiving or moving stock.
2. Choose the movement type, enter the quantity, and include a reason or reference when requested.
3. Review the available quantity and confirm the movement.
4. Use the movement history and low-stock information to check the updated balance.

Bulk and serialized stock are separate. A single hire cannot mix the two tracking types. Reserved, damaged, and inspection stock is not available for ordinary use.

### Cycle counts and inspections

To count serialized gowns:

1. Open **Inventory → Cycle Count** and choose the branch.
2. Scan or enter every gown physically present, then submit the count.
3. Ask a different authorized manager to review and approve the differences. Unscanned gowns are not marked missing until approval.

To count bulk stock:

1. Open the bulk quantity count page and choose the branch.
2. Enter the physical quantity for each listed product.
3. Submit the count for a second manager to review before any adjustment changes the balance.

To inspect a returned gown:

1. Open the item that is marked **Awaiting inspection**.
2. Check its condition and record whether it can return to available stock or needs repair.
3. Keep a damaged or missing item unavailable until the issue is resolved.

### Bulk sales

To sell bulk stock:

1. Choose **Sell bulk stock** from the inventory area.
2. Select the customer, branch, products, and quantities.
3. Review the amount and confirm payment as requested. Only available stock can be sold.

To record a sale return:

1. Find the original sale in the recent sales list.
2. Enter the returned product, quantity, condition, reason, and refund details if applicable.
3. Send returned units for inspection before making them available again.

### Locations and transfers

Open **Locations & Transfers** to move serialized gowns between branches.

1. Start a transfer and choose the sending branch and receiving branch.
2. Scan or enter the available gown barcodes and review the transfer list.
3. Submit the transfer for the required review. An authorized manager approves and dispatches it.
4. Once dispatched, the gowns show as **In transit** and are not available to issue.
5. Staff at the receiving branch open the transfer and scan the gowns as they arrive. Check any difference before completing receipt.
6. If a gown was scanned by mistake, resolve the draft before approval. Repeated scans do not create duplicate movements.

## Hiring, reservations, and returns

### Hiring register

1. Open **Hiring & Returns** to see current hires, reservations, return dates, customers, items, and status.
2. Use search and status filters to find a hire. Issued records show return actions or the next step available to your role.
3. For a reservation, choose an available handover or cancellation action if your role allows it.
4. Open **Issue gowns** to start a walk-in hire or **Reserve gowns** to plan a later handover.

### Issue gowns

1. Open **Hiring & Returns → Issue gowns**.
2. Search for and select a customer. If no result appears, choose **Add customer** to create a profile and return to the hire form.
3. Select the pickup branch and the expected return date. Link a graduation event if one applies.
4. Choose serialized gowns or bulk quantity, depending on how the stock is tracked.
5. For serialized stock, scan or enter each barcode. The availability result tells you whether the gown can be issued at that branch. Remove or resolve any unavailable item before continuing.
6. For bulk stock, choose each product and quantity, then add it to the issue list.
7. Review the customer, items, branch, due date, and charges, then select **Confirm gown issue**.
8. Wait for the success confirmation before handing over the gowns. If the page returns to the form without confirmation, check for messages and review the hire list before trying again.

### Reserve gowns for later

1. Choose **Reserve gowns** from the hiring page.
2. Select the customer, branch, expected handover or event details, and serialized barcodes or bulk quantities.
3. Review the reservation and confirm it.
4. At handover, open the reservation and choose **Handover**.
5. Scan each reserved gown or confirm the counted bulk quantities. The whole reservation must be checked before the system records the issue.

**Note:** A reservation holds stock until it is handed over or cancelled. If a deposit has already been received, it may need to be fully refunded before cancellation.

### Returns

1. Open **Hiring & Returns → Scan Return**.
2. Choose the receiving branch and scan or enter the gown barcode.
3. Confirm the hire and customer shown on screen.
4. Select **Good** or **Damaged** for an item that physically came back. Add inspection notes where useful.
5. If the gown did not come back, select the missing option and explain the discrepancy. Do not mark a missing gown as physically returned.
6. For bulk returns, choose the bulk hire line and enter the returned quantity, condition, and receiving branch.
7. Confirm the return and check the on-screen result.
8. Send a physically returned gown for inspection before treating it as available for another hire. A missing report removes the gown from availability and requires a manager to review any write-off.
9. Review late charges using the terms recorded when the gown was issued. The final amount is fixed at physical return. A manager can review a waiver request, which may need approval from a different authorized person.

## Payments and finance

### Payments overview

1. Open **Payments → Overview** to see shortcuts and high-level totals.
2. Choose the separate page for receipts, deposits, penalties and refunds, or operating expenses.

### Record and download receipts

1. Open **Payments → Receipts** and choose whether the payment is for a gown hire or custom order.
2. Search for and select the correct hire/order and customer.
3. Choose the receipt purpose and payment method, enter the amount, and add the provider reference when applicable.
4. Review the details and select **Record receipt**.
5. In the receipt register, use **PDF** or **Thermal** on the correct row to download a customer copy. Use **Download receipts** to export the available receipt list.

Manual receipt entry records a payment that has already been received; it does not send money or start a mobile-money payment. A customer PIN prompt is available only when the payment service is configured. **Note:** Verify the customer, hire/order, amount, and reference before saving.

### Deposits

1. Open **Payments → Deposits** to review unallocated refundable deposits.
2. Choose **Apply deposit** on an eligible row.
3. Select the correct invoiced hire or order, receipt, and amount, then confirm.
4. Check that the amount does not exceed the available deposit or balance due. A deposit remains separate from earned income until it is applied or refunded.

### Penalties and refunds

1. Open **Payments → Penalties & refunds** and review outstanding penalties and the amount accruing.
2. To request a waiver, choose the hire, enter the amount and a clear reason, then submit it for approval. A separate authorized approver can review the request.
3. To record a refund, choose the original receipt, enter the refund amount and method, and explain the reason.
4. Complete any cash, bank, or mobile-money transfer through the appropriate payment service outside GOWNSEA. This screen records the refund but does not transfer money.

**Warning:** Refunds and payment records affect financial totals. Select the original receipt carefully and verify the amount before submitting.

### Operating expenses

1. Open **Payments → Expenses**.
2. Enter the branch, category, amount, expense date, vendor if known, and a short description.
3. Add the receipt reference or attach a JPG, PNG, or PDF if available.
4. Select **Submit for approval**.
5. A different authorized manager reviews a pending expense and approves or rejects it with a reason.
6. After approval, an authorized finance or manager user records how and when it was paid.

**Note:** Use **Vendor Bills** for supplier invoices, amounts due later, and partial payments. The expense form is for smaller operating costs paid as a single item.

### Finance overview, invoices, vendor bills, and ledger

1. Open **Finance overview** to review income, held deposits, late-fee balances, expenses, and links to detailed finance pages.
2. Open **Customer invoices** to review hire or custom-order invoices and balances. Select a document to download its PDF. Credit notes appear where an invoice has been reduced or corrected.
3. Open **Vendor Bills** to enter a supplier invoice, branch, dates, and amount. Record one or more payments against its remaining balance. If a bill is linked to stock or gown assets, check that the selected items match the supplier's invoice.
4. Open **General ledger** to review dated account activity and branch references. An authorized finance or manager user can reverse eligible entries with a reason. Ask finance or inventory management for help correcting posted stock movements.
5. If a tax invoice service status is shown, read its message before retrying. Only an authorized finance user should use **Queue eTIMS retry**, after checking the invoice and the message on screen.

## Reports

1. Open **Reports** to review stock totals, inventory by status, and available operational downloads.
2. Choose a branch or leave **All accessible locations** selected.
3. Set the date range. Use the customer filter for reports that support customer activity.
4. Select **Apply filters**.
5. In **Operational reports**, choose **PDF** for a printable copy or **CSV** (a spreadsheet file) to open the rows in a spreadsheet program.
6. Use the **Financial reconciliation** section to review receipts, refunds, deposits applied, expenses, receivables, vendor balances, and other totals for the selected period.

Some totals are current balances while others follow the selected dates; read the description under each figure. Customer filters do not change the overall financial reconciliation. These management reports help with day-to-day review and are not, by themselves, a complete official accounting or tax report.

## Graduation events

Open **Graduation Events** to review graduation dates, collections, deliveries, hire dates, returns, fittings, and order deadlines in a month calendar.

1. Use the left and right arrows to change months.
2. Hover a day to see it highlighted. If you have event management access, click an empty area of a day to open **Add an event** with that date already selected.
3. Enter the event name, institution, branch, date, expected gown quantity, and any venue or collection/delivery dates.
4. Save the event. Use the event list below the calendar to review or edit an existing graduation event.
5. Point to an event to read its details in the tooltip. The calendar also shows operational dates created by other work, such as hires and returns.

The expected quantity helps planning; it does not automatically reserve stock.

## Institutions, staff, and settings

### Institutions

1. Open **Institutions** to view education partners.
2. Check spelling and search for an existing institution before adding one, because customers and leads may already be linked to that record.
3. Select the institution form, enter the requested name and contact details, and save.
4. To update a partner later, use the edit action on its row and save the changes.

### Staff access and roles

Open **Roles & Permissions** to review the staff accounts, operational roles, and branch assignments available to your account.

1. Select a staff member or use **Add staff** to begin an invitation.
2. Enter their name, email, role, active status, assigned branches, and primary branch.
3. Save the changes. New staff receive a one-time setup link by email.
4. To change access later, update the staff member and save again.
5. To manage operational roles, open the role controls, create or update a role, and choose the work areas it can access. A super administrator controls the highest-level management accounts.

Access changes what a person can view and do. Only give the branches and actions the person needs. A deactivated staff member cannot continue using the app.

### Settings

Open **Settings** to manage the parts of the workspace available to administrators. Settings are grouped into categories such as organization and access, operating policies, finance and compliance, and integrations/recovery.

1. Open **Settings** and choose a category such as organization and access, operating policies, finance and compliance, or integrations/recovery.
2. Under **Locations**, add or update a warehouse or branch, its code, name, type, contact details, time zone, and address. Treat location codes as permanent references.
3. Under **Late return policy**, set the daily fee, grace period, maximum fee, and whether the return day is charged. New hires use the terms in effect when created; changing settings does not rewrite an existing hire.
4. Under **Business and tax profile**, enter the legal/business details used on receipts and finance documents. A saved draft may require approval from another authorized person before use.
5. Under **Academic gown classifications**, update the display names and availability of listed gown categories.
6. Under **Company profile and receipts**, maintain approved details used on printed receipts. Match the legal company name, KRA PIN, address, phone, and footer to official records. A draft may need a separate authorized approver before it appears on documents.
7. Update **Finance, integrations, and recovery settings** only when authorized and when the supporting account or approval details are available. If a setting is unfamiliar, ask the finance or system administrator before changing it.

## Account security

Choose **Account security** from the account menu to manage sign-in protection.

1. To enable two-step sign-in, select the authenticator setup option and scan the displayed square code with an authenticator app.
2. Enter the current code from that app to confirm setup.
3. Save the recovery codes somewhere private. Each code can be used if you lose access to the authenticator app.
4. Use **Sign out other sessions** if you need to end other active sign-ins while keeping this device signed in.
5. To turn off two-step sign-in, use the disable option and confirm as requested.

Never share a password, authenticator code, recovery code, or password setup link with another person.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot find a branch, customer, gown, or hire

1. Check spelling, barcode, filters, and the selected branch.
2. If the record still does not appear, ask a manager to confirm your branch access and the record's current location or status.

### A barcode says the gown is unavailable

1. Check that the code was scanned correctly.
2. Review the displayed status; the gown may be hired, reserved, in transit, damaged, awaiting inspection, missing, or assigned to another branch.
3. Do not issue it until the stock record has been reviewed.

### A form returns without a success message

1. Check the whole page for highlighted fields or a banner.
2. Confirm the internet connection, correct branch, required details, and item availability.
3. For an issue, return, receipt, or refund, check the relevant register before submitting again so you do not create a duplicate.

### A record is waiting for approval

1. Identify which count, expense, waiver, quotation, or business setting is pending.
2. Ask an eligible manager or finance user to open the relevant page and review it.

### I cannot see a page or button

1. Check whether the page or action is limited to your role or assigned branches.
2. Ask an administrator to check your access. Do not use another person's account.

### I need help with an incorrect record

1. Note the record reference, branch, date, and barcode if applicable.
2. Contact your manager or finance lead with those details.
3. Do not try to repair an incorrect financial or stock history by entering a second unrelated transaction.
