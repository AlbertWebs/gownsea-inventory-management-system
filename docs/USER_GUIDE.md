# Staff quick guide

This guide covers workflows currently available in the application. Consult [implementation progress](IMPLEMENTATION_PROGRESS.md) for deployment prerequisites, provider verification and release gates.

## Graduation events

Users with management or sales/CRM access can open **Graduation Events** to create or update events for their assigned branches. Store the institution, expected gown count, venue, event date and collection/delivery dates. Changes are audited; only planned and confirmed events appear on the dashboard's upcoming list. Use the month calendar to review graduations, collections, deliveries, linked hires, due returns, fittings and custom-order deadlines. When creating a hire, select a graduation event matching the customer's institution, pickup branch and event date to retain an explicit booking link. Capacity checks against expected demand are not yet available.

## CRM leads

Open **Customers & CRM** to create a lead, link an existing customer and/or institution, set its stage, estimated value, next action and follow-up time. A Lost lead requires a reason. Kenyan phone numbers are stored in E.164 form. Open a lead's **Conversation** history to record a call, email, meeting or note; entries are timestamped, branch-scoped and auditable. Schedule follow-up tasks with a title, due time, and optional staff assignee; staff must be assigned to the lead's branch. Open tasks are ordered by due date, overdue tasks are highlighted, and completion is recorded in the lead history. The scheduled reminder service queues one email for each due open task assigned to active staff. Move a qualified lead to **Won**, then use **Convert to customer**; the conversion retains the lead history and creates a branch assigned customer profile. If a matching phone or email already exists, link that existing customer before conversion to prevent a duplicate. Transactional SMS reminders are queued for hire issue, completed return, due-tomorrow and first-day-overdue events when customer SMS consent is active and Techrise is configured.

Open **Quotations** from the CRM page to prepare an itemized draft for an open lead. Drafts can be revised; each revision and its line items remain in history. Management must approve a revision before it can be sent or accepted; changing price, validity or notes resets approval. Sending queues an email with the matching revision PDF when an email address is available. Record acceptance or decline with a reason. For each line, choose its fulfillment type; hire and sale lines must map to the matching active product and tracking type. An accepted quote can be converted once: custom-order lines create a custom order, bulk-hire lines reserve mapped quantities for handover, serialized-hire lines reserve the scanned quoted assets, and bulk-sale lines issue stock and record full payment at the accepted quote prices. Bulk sale requires payment confirmation before submission, and M-Pesa requires its provider receipt reference. Split offers into separate quotes when they need different fulfillment workflows. Tax remains unconfigured.

## Custom orders

Use **Custom Orders** to record made-to-order graduation wear for a customer assigned to the chosen branch. Enter the item, size/color, measurements as a JSON object of text values, design specifications, quantity, agreed total, required deposit amount, expected completion date and delivery method. The register shows the agreed charge paid and due plus required deposit held and due. The deposit field records the required amount; it does not record receipt. Status changes follow the order lifecycle and require a reason, with the actor and before/after order summary retained in the timeline and audit log. Overdue is computed from the due date while the order is open. Production staff can update status and courier tracking but cannot change approved specifications, pricing or cancel orders. An order cannot be cancelled while an unrefunded deposit remains held; refund it through **Sales & Payments** first. Finance can apply available held deposit receipts to that order's charge from **Sales & Payments**; applications reduce the charge balance and held-deposit liability, count toward earned revenue, and remain in an append-only audit ledger. Add measurement, cutting, sewing, embroidery, fitting, alteration, quality check or packaging tasks, then assign each to active staff with access to the branch and set its due date. Task changes require a reason and are also recorded in the order history. Schedule fitting appointments with an assigned fitter; record attendance, outcomes and updated measurements. Fitting changes are audited and added to the order history, and appointment reminders are queued for email. After dispatch, log courier pickup, transit, attempted delivery, delivery, delays or exceptions against the tracking number; updates are idempotent and appear in the order history. Consent-gated SMS updates can be queued at confirmation, ready-for-collection, dispatch and delivery. Record materials, labor, delivery and other direct costs for the order. The contribution report compares agreed value with recorded direct costs; it is a management estimate and not accounting profit.

## Sign in and locations

Sign in with the staff account created by an administrator. Branch accounts only see records within assigned locations. If a page has no available location, contact an administrator to review the assignment. Use the online indicator as the source of truth: the PWA does not queue stock or payment changes while offline.

Authorized administrators can create staff accounts and update roles, active status, assigned locations and primary branch in **Settings → Staff accounts and branch access**. New staff get a secure password setup link by email. Access changes are audited. Management users can create and manage operational roles; only a super administrator can grant management or super-administrator access. Deactivated users are signed out when they next make a request. Password recovery uses the same email delivery; production mail and queue worker configuration are required. Finance, management and super-administrator accounts can enable authenticator-based two-factor authentication and revoke other database-backed sessions from account security settings.

Management can update the default daily late-return fee, grace period, cap and whether the return date counts in **Settings → Late return policy**. Each new hire/reservation snapshots the policy. Existing hires keep their original terms. Finance shows a return-today estimate for issued gowns; the estimate is not payable until physical return finalizes the assessment. Product, institution and contract-reference overrides are supported, with the most specific applicable policy taking priority.

## Register opening stock

Open **Inventory → Register stock**, enter a unique product SKU and product information, select the physical location where the items have been counted, and scan or paste up to 200 unique asset codes. A successful save creates one product and one serialized unit per code, and records receipt/custody history. Reconcile the printed labels and physical count before hiring stock.

For a periodic branch count of serialized gowns, open **Inventory → Cycle count**, select the location, and scan or paste every asset code physically present there. The submission stores the system's expected stock snapshot and goes to a different manager for review. The manager checks the scanned/expected totals and records an approval reason; only then are unscanned gowns marked missing. If stock moved or changed state after the count began, approval is blocked and staff should submit a fresh count. Another manager can later approve a write-off if the gown cannot be recovered.

Select an asset barcode in the Inventory table to open its product, condition, current custody and movement timeline. Branch users can review assets currently at their location or with custody history at one of their assigned locations.

To make or replace barcode stickers, select assets on the Inventory page (up to the current page of 25), choose **Print labels**, and print the Code 128 labels from the browser. The printable sheet includes the GOWNSEA mark, product/SKU, optional size and human-readable asset code. Staff can scan the barcode with the existing scanner workflows; printing is restricted to assets visible in the user's assigned locations. Confirm printer scale and label stock in a test print before printing a large set.

## Transfer between locations

Create a draft with a source, destination and scanned available asset codes. A manager approves and dispatches; each asset becomes in transit and has no confirmed current location. Destination staff scan the received items to confirm receipt. Repeated scans do not create duplicate movements. Investigate count discrepancies before completing the receipt.

## Issue gowns and process returns

Issue flow selects an existing customer and pickup location, scans or pastes up to 200 serialized asset codes, and records the expected return and optional event date. The system checks availability for the full batch and records customer custody in one transaction. If any item is unavailable, the issue does not partially apply. See below for advance reservations and the separate receipt flow.

For advance planning, choose **Reserve gowns**. Select **Serialized gowns** to hold scanned assets, or **Bulk quantity** to hold requested quantities at that branch so another hire or transfer cannot use them. At handover, open the reservation; scan every serialized gown or confirm the counted bulk quantities to issue the complete batch. An authorized sales/management user may cancel a reservation without a deposit. If a deposit was recorded, finance or management must record a full refund before cancellation. Reservations currently hold stock until staff issue or cancel them; event-date expiry rules are not configured.

At return, choose the receiving branch and scan the asset. Mark a physically returned item as good or damaged; a good return remains pending inspection and is not available for hire until inspection passes. Damaged items stay unavailable for repair/write-off handling. If an issued gown was not physically returned, select **Not returned; report as missing** and enter the reason. The asset is removed from stock availability, and a different manager must approve write-off from Inventory. Missing and write-off events appear in the custody/movement history; the hire remains open until the missing item is resolved.

## Customers

Create a customer with a name, optional contact information, optional institution and assigned branch. Kenyan local mobile numbers are normalized to `+254`. Customer and hire history is scoped by assigned location.

## Record a hire receipt

Authorized finance, management and sales/CRM staff can record a manual receipt against an issued hire or a custom order from **Sales & Payments**. Hire service-charge receipts cannot exceed the remaining invoiced amount after refunds and allocations. Custom order charge receipts cannot exceed the remaining invoiced amount; deposit receipts cannot exceed the required deposit. Authorized finance or management staff can record an append-only refund against a receipt, up to its remaining balance. Finance can apply an available deposit receipt to its own hire or custom order invoice; applications are idempotent, audited and reconciled. Deposits stay outside earned revenue until applied to charges. Finance can submit branch operating expenses; a different management user must approve them before approved expense totals include them. Late-fee policy and return-today estimates are described above. When Daraja is enabled, finance/management/sales users can request a customer PIN prompt for an outstanding hire or custom order balance. The payment remains pending until Safaricom's callback confirms the amount, phone and unique receipt; uncertain callbacks require manual reconciliation. Manual M-Pesa receipt entry remains available for reconciliation. Receipts cannot be allocated across different hires or orders.

Finance can review the date-filtered **Financial reconciliation** panel in **Reports**. It summarizes charge receipts, refunds, deposit applications, custom-order direct costs, approved expenses, period depreciation, receivables, remaining deposit liability, current vendor payables and overdue vendor balances. The **Vendor payable aging and statements** CSV/PDF includes invoices open at period end plus invoices issued during the selected period, payments to date, balances, settlement state and aging buckets. Reports can be filtered to a branch; the Customer activity selector also filters supported hire, payment, balance, CRM and custom-order exports. Customer filtering does not apply to the aggregate financial reconciliation. Download the matching CSV or PDF for a period snapshot. Attach an image or PDF receipt when submitting a small expense through the approval workflow; once approved, finance can mark it paid with its method, reference and payment date.

Use **Sales & Payments → Vendor bills** for supplier invoices that need payable tracking. Record the supplier invoice number, branch, invoice and due dates, and amount. Choose an expense account for an operating cost, attach a received bulk-stock movement to match its exact previously capitalized total, or select the barcoded hire assets on a serialized asset invoice. A receipt or asset invoice does not capitalize the same asset twice. Record partial payments with method, reference and date; the screen caps payments to the remaining balance and posts them against supplier payables. Current balances and past-due payables appear in the Reports reconciliation.

When registering serialized products, enter the unit purchase cost and expected useful life in months. New barcode units capitalize at that cost. Book straight-line depreciation assumes zero residual value and posts monthly on the first day for the preceding period; re-running a period is safe. The reconciliation lists depreciation separately from its cash-basis management result. These figures are not statutory tax depreciation or a complete accrual profit-and-loss statement. See the General ledger for posted account activity.

Open **Sales & Payments → General ledger** for the immutable double-entry journal. It posts receipts, refunds, deposit applications, invoices, inventory valuation, vendor bills, vendor settlements and hire asset depreciation to a system chart of accounts, with balanced debit/credit lines, branch attribution and date filtering. Management, finance and super administrators can reverse eligible entries by reason; source-driven inventory postings are corrected through stock operations. At accounting cutover, run `php artisan gownsea:invoice-backfill --dry-run` and have finance review the listed historical amounts, customers, branches and dates against the approved opening-balance schedule. Only after sign-off should an operator run `php artisan gownsea:invoice-backfill --confirm-reviewed`; the command allocates existing receipts/refunds/deposit applications and reclassifies already-posted hire revenue to receivables. Some other revenue remains collection-basis; statutory tax treatments are not configured, so the management reconciliation is not a complete accrual profit-and-loss statement.

## Bulk inventory

Register fungible stock from **Inventory → Register stock** by choosing **Bulk quantity**, selecting a branch and entering the opening quantity when authorized. Bulk products track integer on-hand, reserved, awaiting-inspection and damaged quantities by location. In the bulk stock panel, receive stock, transfer quantities between assigned locations, or have a manager record a reasoned adjustment. Transfers and receipts are idempotent; the system prevents adjustments that would reduce available quantity below reserved, awaiting-inspection or damaged stock. Review low-stock alerts and recent movements, or export the bulk balance/movement reports.

To fulfill a quantity hire immediately, open **Hiring → Issue gowns**, choose **Bulk quantity**, select the customer, pickup location and available bulk products, then submit the quantities. For a future handover, create a **Bulk quantity** reservation instead. Bulk and serialized units are issued through separate hires; one submission cannot mix both tracking types. On return, open **Hiring & Returns → Scan Return**, choose the active bulk hire line, enter the counted quantity and condition, and select the receiving location. Good returns go into pending inspection; damaged returns stay unavailable in the damaged count. An authorized inventory manager uses **Inventory → Bulk stock inspection** to release passed items or move repaired items back to available stock. Partial return quantities and penalties are retained in an idempotent ledger.

For a bulk stocktake, use **Inventory → Bulk quantity cycle count**, select a branch and enter the physical on-hand count for every active bulk product. Submission snapshots expected quantities without altering stock. A different manager reviews the count and approves differences; approval rechecks stock, reservations and inspection/damage quantities so a stale count cannot overwrite intervening work. Each adjustment is written to the movement and audit ledgers. The counter or an authorized manager can cancel a pending count with a reason and restart it.

For a counter sale of bulk stock, choose **Sell bulk stock** from Inventory. Select the customer, branch, products and quantities; the checkout charges the configured sale price and records full payment at the time of sale. Only available quantity can be sold. For a return, find the sale in the recent sales list and record the product, quantity, condition, reason and refund reference when applicable. Returned units go to pending inspection or damaged stock and are not available until an authorized inspection releases them. This workflow does not support credit/partial-payment sales.

Late returns are assessed when each gown is scanned back. Management can configure the default terms in **Settings**, with environment values used as the initial fallback. Product, institution and contract-reference overrides are supported; the most specific matching rule wins in that order, followed by the default. Each new hire or reservation snapshots its policy, and a calculation record is retained in the audit trail. Finance can record receipts against the unpaid assessed amount. Management can approve a partial or full waiver with a reason; the original assessment remains in history. Fees accrue at physical return; daily live accrual while gowns remain out and customer deposit settlement are not available.

Quotation drafts can include up to five private supporting files per revision (PDF, common image, Word or Excel formats; 10 MB per file). Files are downloaded through the authenticated quotation page and remain restricted to staff with access to that branch. Accepted offers can be converted through the matching fulfillment workflow described above. Serialized hire conversion requires staff to scan the exact quoted asset count at the quotation branch; bulk-hire conversion creates a reservation that must be handed over through the standard reservation flow.

Finance reports include a journal-based partial income statement and custom-order contribution estimates. The order contribution report groups orders by order date and includes direct costs recorded through the selected period end. These figures are management reports; statutory tax treatment and external reconciliation remain subject to configuration and review. For an STK request with a checkout ID, finance can use **Check status** in M-Pesa activity. A successful status query remains in review until the callback supplies receipt details; requests without a checkout ID need manual provider reconciliation.

## SMS and backup operations

SMS messages are disabled until a manager or sales/CRM user records explicit consent on the customer row in **Customers & CRM**. Withdrawing consent immediately suppresses future messages. Hire issue/return confirmations, daily due/overdue reminders, and custom-order confirmation, ready-for-collection, dispatch and delivery updates use the consent-gated Techrise outbox; a worker must be running. Consent changes and message outcomes are audited. Do not manually resend an uncertain provider request until it has been reconciled with Techrise.

Administrators can create an encrypted database/private-file backup with `php artisan gownsea:backup`; scheduled backups run daily when `GOWNSEA_BACKUP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is set. Store the encryption key separately from the configured backup directory. To restore, take the app offline and run `php artisan gownsea:restore <backup-filename>`; the command first makes a pre-restore backup and asks for confirmation. Perform restore drills in an isolated environment and verify records and financial balances before returning to service. See [deployment backup and integration runbook](DEPLOYMENT.md).

## Escalations

Use the record reference and current barcode when contacting management about a stock discrepancy. Do not edit operational records directly in the database. Missing-gown write-off, configurable role-bounded permissions and session revocation are supported in their respective inventory and security pages. Cross-document payment allocation is not supported.
